# AI policy

We welcome the thoughtful use of AI tools when contributing to `forges`, as long as you write in your own voice and understand what you submit.

The policy lives in the documentation: [forges.link/contributing/ai-policy](https://forges.link/contributing/ai-policy).

It is based on the [Human Voice policy](https://ai-policy.dev/policies/human-voice/1.0.0). The rest of the [contribution guide](./CONTRIBUTING.md) still applies.
