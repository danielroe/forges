# Contributing

We welcome contributions to `forges` of any size and skill level. Whether you want to fix a typo, report a bug, add support for another forge or improve the documentation, you are helping everyone who builds on the library, and we are happy to guide you through your first pull request. By taking part, you agree to the [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

The contribution guide lives in the documentation:

- [Guidelines](https://forges.link/contributing/guidelines) covers setting up the repository, running the tests, writing documentation, commit messages, pull requests and reporting a problem.
- [Write a provider](https://forges.link/contributing/write-a-provider) explains how to add a forge or an operation.
- [Record fixtures](https://forges.link/contributing/record-fixtures) explains how to record responses from a live forge for the tests.
- [AI policy](https://forges.link/contributing/ai-policy) explains how we welcome AI tools, and why we ask you to write in your own voice and to understand what you submit.
